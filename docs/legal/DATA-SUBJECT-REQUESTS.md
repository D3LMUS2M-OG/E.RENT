# Handling data subject requests

Self-service covers most requests: **Account → Privacy and security** lets a person download their data and delete their account; Account lets them correct their details and notification choices.

For anything else (email or Contact page):
1. **Log it** with date received, requester, type (access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability).
2. **Verify identity**: reply from the account email or phone, or sign in. Do not release data to an unverified person. Never share identity documents by email.
3. **Decide and act** within the time the Data Protection Act and its regulations allow (aim for 7 days to acknowledge and 30 days to complete).
4. **Access / portability**: use the export, and add identity documents and reported-chat evidence by hand if requested.
5. **Deletion**: ask the person to use Delete my account, or run it for them. Staff accounts must be removed from the team first. Check that files were removed from storage.
6. **Correction**: change the profile value, or ask the person to.
7. **Objection / restriction**: record the decision and the reason; if refused, tell the person they can complain to the ODPC.
8. **Reply in writing** and keep the record for 24 months.
