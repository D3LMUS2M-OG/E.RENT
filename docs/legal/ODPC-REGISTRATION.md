# Registering with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)

Do this before public launch. Registration is done online through the ODPC portal (see odpc.go.ke for the current process and fee).

## You will need
- Legal name and registration details of the operator (business name, company, or individual) and a contact person
- Physical and postal address, email and phone
- Description of the purposes of processing (use section 3 of the Privacy Policy)
- Categories of data subjects: tenants, landlords, caretakers and agents, staff
- Categories of personal data: account details, identity documents, chat messages, location of listings, reviews (see Privacy Policy section 2)
- Recipients: Supabase, Vercel, Resend, Google, Meta (WhatsApp, optional), SMS provider, error monitoring
- Transfers outside Kenya: yes (provider regions); safeguards as in Privacy Policy section 7
- Security measures: see `docs/legal/SECURITY-MEASURES.md`
- Retention periods: `docs/legal/RETENTION-SCHEDULE.md`

## After registering
- Put the registration number in the Privacy Policy section 1
- Diary the annual renewal
- Keep the DPIA (`docs/legal/DPIA.md`) and this folder up to date
